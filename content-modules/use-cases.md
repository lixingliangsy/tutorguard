# TutorGuard — Use Cases

### Parents hiring tutors
- **Pain:** Hard to know what to ask in interviews.
- **How TutorGuard helps:** Get a vetting checklist and red-flag list.
### AI tutor products
- **Pain:** Need reply safety before students see content.
- **How TutorGuard helps:** Screen replies for unsafe content signals (product positioning).
### Agencies
- **Pain:** Need consistent quality/safety gates.
- **How TutorGuard helps:** Enterprise shared rulesets.
### Cost fairness
- **Pain:** Unclear pricing or overpromising.
- **How TutorGuard helps:** Fairness warning signs in the report.
